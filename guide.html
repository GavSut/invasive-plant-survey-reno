Quick field terms
Rosette
A circular cluster of leaves at ground level.
Bract
A modified leaf below or around a flower or flower head.
Awn
A stiff bristle extending from a grass floret.
Spikelet
A small unit of a grass seed head containing one or more florets.
Ligule
The small membrane or hairs where a grass blade meets its sheath.
Clasping
A leaf base that partly or fully wraps around the stem.
Samara
A dry, winged fruit such as those on elms or tree-of-heaven.
Annual / biennial / perennial
A life cycle lasting one year, usually two years, or more than two years.
